About Us

PPG Institute of Technology is one of the best engineering colleges in Coimbatore, established under the aegis of the renowned P.Perichi Gounder Memorial Education and Charitable Trust (PPG Trust) in the year 2008. The institution is the brainchild of Dr.L.P.Thangavelu, M.S., who envisioned imparting value education par excellence to students.

Approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi, PPGIT is affiliated to Anna University and is considered one of the top engineering colleges in Tamilnadu. The college offers an erudite atmosphere crafted with ethics and ethos of Indian culture, providing students with a holistic educational experience.

Equipped with excellent facilities and state-of-the-art infrastructure, PPGIT is all geared up to perform and progress, making it one of the best engineering colleges in Coimbatore.
